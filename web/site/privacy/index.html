OpenVector

Privacy

Updated 26 November 2026.

Your images

The editor traces images in your browser. Images you open are never uploaded. They stay in the tab's memory and disappear when you close it. Exports download straight to your device.

Cookies

OpenVector sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser between visits.

Visit statistics

OpenVector uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It records the page, the referring site, your browser and device type, and your country. It uses no cookies, no local storage and no fingerprinting, and it cannot identify you.

Hosting

Cloudflare hosts the site. To deliver pages and block attacks, it processes your IP address and request details. See the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Email

If you email an openvector.app address, your message is used only to reply to you.

Other sites

Links to GitHub, GitHub Sponsors and npm lead to services with their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@openvector.app.
