Is OpenVector free?

Yes. OpenVector is free and open source under the MIT license. There is no account, watermark or export limit.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The tracing engine runs in your browser as WebAssembly. Images and results stay in the tab's memory and are gone when you close it.

Which image and vector formats work?

It opens PNG, JPEG, GIF, BMP and TIFF. It exports SVG, PDF, EPS, AI, DXF and EMF vectors, and PNG, JPEG, BMP and TIFF renderings.

Which images trace well?

Logos, icons, flat illustrations and scanned line art. Photographs trace into thousands of small shapes and rarely make a useful vector.

Which browsers can run OpenVector?

A current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari with WebAssembly threads and WebGL2. The first visit downloads about 5 MB; later visits use the cached copy.
